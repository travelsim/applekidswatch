### Panel 3: Step-by-Step Setup (5 Minutes)

```
STEP 1: POWER ON
────────────────
Press and hold the side button until the Apple logo appears.
Place the watch on its charger to ensure it has enough battery.

STEP 2: OPEN THE WATCH APP
───────────────────────────
On YOUR iPhone, open the Watch app.
Tap "All Watches" at the top left.
Tap "Add Watch" → "Set Up for a Family Member."

STEP 3: PAIR THE WATCH
───────────────────────
Hold your iPhone over the animation on the watch screen.
It will auto-pair. When it asks "Set Up as Family Member" — tap YES.
(This is the most important step!)

STEP 4: CREATE YOUR CHILD'S ACCOUNT
────────────────────────────────────
If your child doesn't have an Apple ID:
  Tap "Create Apple ID for Child"
  Enter their date of birth
  Set up Screen Time passcode (write it down!)
  Agree to Family Sharing

If they already have an Apple ID:
  Sign in with their existing account

STEP 5: SET UP THE WATCH
─────────────────────────
Choose settings:
• Approved Contacts — Who can call/text them
• Schooltime Mode — Set school hours
• Location Sharing — On (for Find My)
• Apple Cash Family — Optional allowance
• Ask to Buy — When they try to download apps

STEP 6: ACTIVATE CELLULAR
─────────────────────────
Tap "Mobile Data" in the Watch app
Choose your carrier:
  • T-Mobile: Add watch to plan ($10/mo)
  • Verizon: Add to your plan (~$10/mo)
  • AT&T: Add to plan ($10/mo)

Follow the on-screen prompts. Setup is complete in 2–3 minutes.

✅  YOU'RE ALL SET!
```

### Back Panel (Tips + Support)

```
PRO TIPS
═════════

🔋  Battery Life
    Charge nightly! Place watch on charger at bedtime.
    Pro tip: Use "Theater Mode" (swipe up → theater icon)
    during school to save battery.

🏫  Schooltime Mode
    Yellow ring means "in school mode." Child sees just the time.
    To set hours: Watch app → Schooltime → Schedule.

📍  Find My
    See your child's location in real-time.
    Open the Find My app on your iPhone → tap Devices.
    Set up Arrival/Departure alerts (geofencing).

🆘  Emergency SOS
    Hold the side button for 2 seconds.
    Watch calls 911 or your local emergency number.
    Emergency contacts are also notified.

🔄  Need a reset?
    Settings → General → Reset → Erase All Content
    Then re-pair following steps 2–6 above.
```

## Part 4: FAQ — Top 10 Setup Questions

| # | Question | Answer |
|---|----------|--------|
| 1 | Does my child need their own iPhone? | No! Family Setup lets you manage everything from YOUR iPhone. The watch works independently. |
| 2 | What carriers work? | T-Mobile, Verizon, and AT&T all support Apple Watch Family Setup. Plans are ~$10/month. |
| 3 | Can the watch call 911? | Yes. Holding the side button for 2 seconds activates Emergency SOS. |
| 4 | How do I see my child's location? | Open the Find My app on your iPhone → tap Devices → select their watch. |
| 5 | Can my child download apps? | Yes, but with Ask to Buy enabled, you approve every download from your phone. |
| 6 | What if the watch breaks? | We offer a 1-year warranty covering defects. Extended warranty ($19.99) covers accidental damage. |
| 7 | Can I limit screen time? | Yes. Use Screen Time in the Watch app on your iPhone. |
| 8 | Does the watch work at school? | Yes. Schooltime Mode silences everything except the clock. The yellow ring is visible to teachers. |
| 9 | What's the battery life? | About 18 hours with typical use on cellular. Charge overnight. |
| 10 | Can grandparents call the watch? | Yes. Add them as Approved Contacts during setup. Their calls/texts come through to the watch. |
