# Board Report — CMO Findings & Go/No-Go Recommendation

**Prepared by:** CMO, AppleKidsWatch
**Date:** May 28, 2026

---

## Executive Summary

After comprehensive market research, competitive analysis, viability pressure-testing, and go-to-market planning, the CMO recommends: **GO — with strategic conditions.**

AppleKidsWatch — selling refurbished Apple Watch SE units configured for kids via Apple's Family Setup — is a viable niche business. The market is growing at 14–20% CAGR, the competitive moat (Apple ecosystem) is real, and the unit economics work at modest volumes (breakeven at ~30–70 units/month).

However, the business faces material risks around supply chain (SE 2 obsolescence), margin compression on entry-level SKUs, and meaningful carrier plan friction for price-sensitive parents.

## 2. Go/No-Go Recommendation

### VERDICT: GO (conditional)

### Conditions for Go:

1. **Lead with GPS+Cellular models.** Cellular is required for Family Setup. GPS-only models won't work for the core use case. This also protects margins (GPS+Cellular has 16–21% gross margin vs 7.5–12% for GPS-only).

2. **Secure supply for 12 months.** Need at least 2 reliable wholesale suppliers. Target blended COGS of $110–$120 for SE 2 GPS+Cellular. Lock in pricing before launch.

3. **Build the SE 3 supply roadmap.** The SE 2 is discontinued. By month 6–9, we need a path to SE 3 refurb units. Without this, the business hits a supply wall.

4. **Keep operations lean.** No office, no employees initially. Solo operation with outsourced refurbishment. Fixed costs under $500/month.

5. **Invest in customer education.** Family Setup requires specific steps. An investment in setup guides, video walkthroughs, and post-purchase email sequences is non-negotiable.

### Go Indicators (All Present)

- ✅ Market growing 14–20% CAGR
- ✅ Apple ecosystem moat is real and defensible
- ✅ Unit economics work at low volumes (30–70 units/month)
- ✅ Low fixed costs ($400–$500/month)
- ✅ Competitors are either expensive (Bounce 2) or lack ecosystem (TickTalk, Gizmo)
- ✅ Clear differentiation: "It's a real Apple Watch, for less"
- ✅ SEO opportunity: many low-competition keywords around Apple Watch for kids
