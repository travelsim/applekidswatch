## Notes & Caveats

- **All search volumes are directional estimates** based on Google Keyword Planner ranges and industry tools. Actual volumes will be refined once GSC data is live (4–6 weeks post-launch).
- **Keyword difficulty scores** assume a brand-new domain. If applekidswatch.com has existing authority from previous content, adjust downward by 1–2 points.
- **Priority Score formula:** `(volume_midpoint × relevance_factor) / difficulty`. Relevance factor: 1.0 for direct product fit, 0.8 for adjacent topics, 0.5 for broad informational terms.
- **Re-evaluate monthly.** Keyword landscape shifts, new competitors enter, and our own data will refine estimates.
- **Monitor Apple's own SEO moves.** If Apple publishes "Apple Watch for Kids" content, difficulty on Tier 1 terms will increase.
