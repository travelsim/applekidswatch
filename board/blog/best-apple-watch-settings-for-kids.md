# Best Apple Watch Settings for Kids: Step-by-Step Guide for Parents

**Setting up an Apple Watch for your child? You've come to the right place. This step-by-step guide walks you through every Apple Watch setting parents need to configure — from Family Setup to Schooltime, Approved Contacts, Screen Time, and Emergency SOS.**

*Estimated read time: 8 minutes*

---

## Why Apple Watch Settings Matter for Kids

Handing your kid an Apple Watch is a big step toward giving them independence — a way to stay connected without handing them a phone. But out of the box, an Apple Watch isn't configured for a child. Without the right parental controls, your child could access unapproved contacts, apps, or get distracted during school hours.

The good news? Apple's **Family Setup** feature gives parents granular control over exactly what their child can do. Here's how to lock it all down, step by step.

## Before You Start: What You'll Need

Before you can configure the settings below, make sure you have:

- **A GPS + Cellular Apple Watch Series 4 or later** (or Apple Watch SE). Cellular is required for Family Setup — the watch needs its own connection to make calls and send texts without a nearby iPhone.
- **An iPhone 6s or later** running the latest iOS — this is the parent's phone that manages the watch.
- **Family Sharing enabled** with your Apple ID as the organizer.
- **An Apple ID for your child** (create one during setup if you haven't already).
- **A cellular plan** for the watch (~$10/mo with most major US carriers).

## Step 1: Family Setup — The Foundation

Family Setup is Apple's system for pairing a watch to a child (or older adult) who doesn't own their own iPhone. Here's how to get started:

### Walkthrough

1. **On your iPhone**, open the **Watch app**.
2. Tap **All Watches** (top-left corner), then tap **Add Watch**.
3. Select **Set Up for a Family Member**.
4. **Position the watch** in the viewfinder to pair it. *(Screenshot: Pairing animation shows a circular viewfinder framing the watch face.)*
5. Choose **Set Up Apple Watch** and tap **Continue**.
6. When asked "Who will use this Apple Watch?", select your child's name from Family Sharing, or tap **Add New Family Member** to create their Apple ID.
7. Review the **Family Setup information screen** — Apple will show you a summary of what works (calls, messages, location sharing) and what doesn't (standalone Apple Pay, some third-party apps).
8. Set a **passcode** for the watch. For younger kids, a simple 4-digit code is fine.
9. Choose **Cellular Setup** — you can activate a new plan or skip if you've already activated one.
10. Wait for the watch to finish syncing. This takes 2-5 minutes.

**Screenshot description:** *The Family Setup welcome screen on iPhone showing "Set Up for a Family Member" option with a silhouette graphic of a parent and child.*

Once paired, the watch appears in your Watch app under your child's name with a special "family member" badge icon.

## Step 2: Schooltime — Distraction-Free Learning

Schooltime is a Focus mode unique to Family Setup watches. When enabled, the watch shows a simple yellow clock face and blocks all apps, notifications, and complications.

### How to Configure Schooltime

1. In the **Watch app** on your iPhone, tap your child's watch.
2. Tap **Schooltime**.
3. Tap **Edit Schedule** to set recurring hours.
4. Tap **Add Schedule**, then choose:
   - **Days of the week** (e.g., Monday–Friday)
   - **Start time** (e.g., 8:00 AM)
   - **End time** (e.g., 3:00 PM)
5. Tap **Add** to save.

**Screenshot description:** *The Schooltime schedule editor showing a Monday-Friday schedule from 8:00 AM to 3:00 PM with a toggle switch enabled.*

### Bonus: How Schooltime Looks on the Watch

When Schooltime is active, the watch displays a bright yellow face with the time and a "Do Not Disturb" crescent icon. Your child can't open apps or respond to notifications. To **temporarily exit Schooltime** (e.g., during lunch), they press the Digital Crown and tap **Exit Schooltime** — but you get a notification on your iPhone when they do.

> [!TIP]
> Don't skip this setting! It's the single most important configuration for school-aged kids. Without it, they'll be buzzing through math class.

## Step 3: Approved Contacts — Who Can Reach Your Child

One of the biggest benefits of Family Setup is that **only approved contacts** can call or message your child. No strangers, no spam, no unknown numbers.

### How to Set Up Approved Contacts

1. In the **Watch app**, tap your child's watch.
2. Tap **Contacts** → **Add Contact**.
3. Choose contacts from your iPhone's address book. Each contact shows their phone number.
4. Tap **Done** when you've selected everyone.

**Screenshot description:** *The Approved Contacts management screen showing a list of family members with green checkmarks next to their names.*

### What This Controls

- Your child can **only call and message** people in this list.
- It applies to both **phone calls and iMessage/SMS**.
- Your child **cannot add new contacts** on the watch — only you can from your iPhone.
- They **can** receive calls and texts from anyone in their approved list, and they can initiate calls/texts to anyone in their approved list.

### Pro Tip: Set the "Primary Contact"

While you're here, scroll down and tap **Primary Contact**. This sets who the watch calls when your child long-presses the side button or says "Hey Siri, call Mom." Pick yourself or your partner.

## Step 4: Screen Time — Set Daily Limits and Downtime

Screen Time on the Apple Watch works similarly to iPhone — you can set total usage limits, app limits, and a "Downtime" window where only essential apps work.

### How to Configure Screen Time

1. On your **iPhone**, go to **Settings** → **Screen Time**.
2. Scroll down to your child's name under **Family**.
3. Tap **App Limits** → **Add Limit**.
4. Select categories to limit (Games, Social, Entertainment) and set a daily time allowance.
5. Tap **Downtime** to set a quiet window (e.g., 8:00 PM – 7:00 AM) when only phone calls and approved apps work.

**Screenshot description:** *Screen Time family settings showing daily limits for Games (30 min) and Social (15 min) with "Block at End of Limit" enabled.*

### Key Screen Time Settings to Toggle

| Setting | Recommended | Why |
|---------|-------------|-----|
| **iTunes & App Store Purchases** | Require Ask to Buy | You approve every app download |
| **Installing Apps** | Block | Prevents unauthorized installs |
| **Deleting Apps** | Block | Stops kids from removing essential apps |
| **Location Services** | Don't Allow Changes | Keeps Find My always on |

> [!WARNING]
> **"Ask to Buy" is essential.** Without it, your child could download apps — including paid ones — without your knowledge. Enable it during Screen Time setup.

## Step 5: Emergency SOS — Safety Net Settings

Every Apple Watch can detect falls and call emergency services, but for a child's watch you'll want to configure these settings specifically.

### How to Set Up Emergency SOS

1. In the **Watch app** → your child's watch → **SOS**.
2. Toggle **Hold Side Button to Call** to ON.
3. Toggle **Call with Hold & Release** if you want a simpler activation (watchOS 10+).
4. Tap **Edit Health ID** to add:
   - Medical conditions (e.g., allergies, asthma)
   - Allergies and medications
   - Emergency contact info
5. Tap **Set Up Emergency Contacts** in the Watch app under your child's watch, or on the iPhone's Health app.

### What Happens When Your Child Triggers SOS

- The watch calls **local emergency services**.
- It then sends an **SMS to all emergency contacts** with the child's location.
- The watch displays the child's **Medical ID** so first responders can see critical info.

**Screenshot description:** *The Emergency SOS settings screen showing "Hold Side Button to Call" toggled ON, with the Emergency Contacts list below showing two contacts.*

### Fall Detection for Active Kids

If your child has an Apple Watch SE or Series 8+, you can also enable **Fall Detection**:

1. Watch app → your child's watch → **SOS** → **Fall Detection**.
2. Choose **Always On** (for active kids who run, bike, or play sports).

## Step 6: Activity Sharing & Fitness Tracking

Family Setup lets you keep an eye on your child's activity without adding them to your own Activity rings.

### How to Set Up Activity Sharing

1. On your child's watch, open the **Activity** app and complete setup.
2. On your **iPhone**, open the **Fitness** app.
3. Tap the **Sharing** tab, then tap the **+** icon.
4. Enter your child's Apple ID email or phone number.
5. Accept the invitation on your child's watch.

**Screenshot description:** *The Fitness app Sharing tab showing a child's Move, Exercise, and Stand rings alongside the parent's rings.*

Once connected, you'll see their daily activity rings right in your Fitness app. You can also send them encouraging messages (or gentle nudges to close their rings).

> [!TIP]
> Kids aged 13+ can also participate in **Activity Competitions** with you. A little friendly competition is great motivation to stay active!

## Step 7: Location Sharing & Find My

Family Setup automatically enables location sharing, but verify these settings to make sure you can always find your child.

### Verification Steps

1. On your **iPhone**, open **Find My** → **People** tab.
2. Confirm your child is listed under "My Location Sharing" or "Family Members."
3. Tap their name — you should see their watch's location on a map.

**Screenshot description:** *Find My app showing a family member's current location on a map with their Apple Watch battery level displayed below.*

### What Else You Can Do

- **Notify when they leave or arrive** — Set location-based alerts (e.g., "Notify me when Sam arrives at school").
- **Get notified if the watch goes offline** — If your child turns off cellular or the battery dies, you'll know.
- **Play a sound** — Perfect for finding a misplaced watch under the couch.

## Step 8: Bonus Settings Worth Configuring

### Apple Cash Family (Allowances)

You can send your child money via Apple Cash that they can spend using the watch's wallet. Set it up:

1. iPhone → **Settings** → **Family** → tap your child's name → **Apple Cash**.
2. Enable **Apple Cash Family**.
3. Set up **approval** so every transaction needs your OK.
4. Send an allowance directly from Messages or Wallet.

### Do Not Disturb / Focus Modes

Beyond Schooltime, you can set additional Focus modes:

- **Bedtime** — Automatically dims the watch and hides notifications during sleep hours.
- **Custom Focus** — Create one for homework time or extracurricular activities.

### Manage Apps on the Watch

1. Watch app → your child's watch.
2. Scroll to **Installed Apps** and tap **Install** or **Remove**.
3. For music, use **Add Music** to sync playlists for offline listening.
