## Garmin: Bounce 2 vs vívofit jr. 4 (AU correct model)

**Bounce 2 is the correct current kids model for AU.** Garmin AU lists Bounce™ 2 as its "Kids Smartwatch"
(category: Kids Smartwatches), launched/announced 2025-09-18, RRP $449 AUD, LTE calling/texting + real-time
tracking. vívofit jr. is the older activity-tracker line (no LTE watch phone) and has no current AU kids-watch
hero equivalent; catalogue references to vívofit jr. 4 should be treated as placeholder/legacy and replaced by
Bounce 2 if the shop aims to match current AU retail. Bounce 2 colours: Slate Grey `010-03399-00`, plus
`010-03399-01` / `-02` variants.
